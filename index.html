04 AI Lab

Learning by making

Experiments with a purpose.

I use emerging tools as working materials, not résumé decoration. The goal is to understand behavior, limitations, failure modes, and practical value.

Multi-model workflow

I work across AI systems instead of treating one model as the answer to every problem. Codex supports planning, coding, debugging, and workflow orchestration; local Llama models through Ollama support private, on-device inference experiments; and ComfyUI provides a visual, node-based environment for building and refining Stable Diffusion and Flux image workflows. I compare outputs, move useful context between tools, and choose each model for the part of the process it handles best.

  • Codex for implementation, debugging, and technical orchestration
  • Google AI Studio, ChatGPT, and Gemini for model comparison and exploration
  • Ollama and local Llama models for local inference
  • ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion, and Flux for visual generation workflows
  • Prompt engineering and AI-assisted application development
